Wellbite Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Wellbite (also listed as Basic Health Tracker) is a simple food and calorie logger. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone, and there is no Wellbite account or server.

What stays on your device

The foods, meals and calories you log are saved in a database on your device. The developer cannot see them. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Health data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

If you turn it on in Settings, Wellbite asks permission to read your calories burned, weight and height from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). This access is read-only: the app never writes to Health.

  • The data is used only to show your calories burned, your net calories, and your latest weight and height on your screen.
  • It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, not used for advertising or marketing, and not sold.
  • You can turn this off in Settings, or revoke access at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect.

Optional AI calorie estimates

If you choose to use the AI feature, the text you type, or the meal photo you pick, is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you set up (Anthropic, OpenRouter, or an endpoint you enter) to get a calorie estimate. This happens only when you ask for an estimate. You supply your own API key, which is kept in your device's secure storage and sent only to that provider. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to what you send. Free models on some providers may log requests, so send only food information. Your Health data is never sent to an AI provider.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to look up the product. No other personal information is sent.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and take meal photos. Photos are used only if you choose to send one for an AI estimate, as described above. They are not otherwise stored or shared by the app.

Children

Wellbite is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes
